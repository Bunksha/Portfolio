Personal Project — Local AI

Dual-GPU Local AI Workstation

I built a self-hosted AI workstation around two passive NVIDIA Tesla V100s — 64GB of VRAM — cooled by a custom 20° fan shroud I designed and 3D-printed for the cards. It runs open-weight models locally with Ollama (a 27B model at a 262,000-token context window) and ComfyUI for image generation, all on my own hardware. It's the testbed behind my AI documentation experiments.
