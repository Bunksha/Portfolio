Local AI

Dual-GPU Local AI Workstation

I built a self-hosted AI workstation around two passive NVIDIA Tesla V100s — 64GB of VRAM — cooled by a custom 20° fan shroud I designed and 3D-printed for the cards. It runs open-weight models locally with Ollama (a 27B model at a 262,000-token context window) and ComfyUI for image generation, all on my own hardware — and the shroud holds both cards through 12-plus-hour days of inference and coding-agent sessions. It's the testbed behind my AI documentation experiments.

Tooling — Style Guide Checker

Second Pass

A from-scratch recreation, on my own time and with no company material, of a style-checking concept I first built at work. This version runs Python rules against big published style guides like Microsoft's; the rules are tested against real guide text, and my local AI setup helps me iterate on them. Still in active development.

Hardware Build

Wrist-Mounted Cyberdeck

A computer that lives on my left forearm: a Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB) mounted to a 7-inch touchscreen on a hinge, so the display flips up when I need it and folds flat when I don't. The forearm saddle — foam-lined, held on with straps — carries an integrated power bank and a sliding tray for a folding Bluetooth keyboard. Every mount is designed and 3D-printed by me; currently at the fit-test stage for the saddle.
